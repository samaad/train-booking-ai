# Fares and travel classes

## How fares are set
Every fare is calculated when you book, from a fixed amount plus a rate per kilometre for the distance you travel. The fare depends on the train, the class and your boarding and destination stations. The assistant cannot quote fares from memory; the price shown on your booking is the only authoritative price.

## Business class
Business class offers wider seats in a 2+1 layout, at-seat power, a complimentary snack and priority boarding. Business seats are numbered with a B, for example B4.

## Economy class
Economy class offers comfortable seats in a 2+2 layout with shared power sockets. Economy seats are numbered with an E, for example E12.

## Children
Children under four travel free on an adult's lap and do not need a seat. Older children need their own ticket.
