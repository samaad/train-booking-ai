# Accessibility

All stations have step-free access from the street to the platform, with lifts and tactile paving.

## Wheelchair spaces
Every train has two wheelchair spaces in the Economy carriage nearest the accessible toilet. A companion may sit next to the wheelchair space.

## Assistance
Passengers who need help boarding can request assistance at the station information desk at least thirty minutes before departure. Staff will meet you and help you to your seat, and arrange help at your destination.
