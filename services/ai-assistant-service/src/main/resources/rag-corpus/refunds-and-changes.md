# Cancellations, refunds and holds

## Seat holds
When you choose a seat it is held for you for a few minutes while you confirm. If you do not confirm in time the hold expires and the seat becomes available to others. A hold is not a booking.

## Confirming a booking
A booking is only made when you confirm it yourself in the app. The assistant can prepare a booking for you, but it never books or charges you on its own.

## Cancelling
You can cancel a booking before departure in the app or by asking the assistant, which will show you a cancellation card to confirm. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Missed trains
If you miss your train, your ticket is not valid on a later service. Please make a new booking.
