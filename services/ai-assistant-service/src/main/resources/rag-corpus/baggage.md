# Baggage, bicycles and pets

## Baggage allowance
Each passenger may bring two large bags and one small item such as a handbag or laptop bag. Large bags must fit in the luggage racks at the end of each carriage. Business passengers may bring one additional large bag.

## Bicycles
Folded bicycles are carried free as luggage. Unfolded bicycles are allowed only on the Western Line outside the morning peak, in the marked bicycle area.

## Pets
Small pets in a closed carrier that fits on your lap are welcome. Assistance animals travel free on every service and may sit with their owner.

## Prohibited items
Flammable materials, gas cylinders and weapons are not allowed on board.
