# Stations and their other names

The network serves six stations. Each has a short code used in searches and on tickets.

## Al Ruwais (RUW)
The western terminus, serving the Ruwais industrial area. Passengers also call it Ruwais or Ar Ruways.

## Al Mirfa (MRF)
A coastal stop between Al Ruwais and Abu Dhabi. Also written Mirfa or Al Marfa.

## Abu Dhabi (AUH)
The capital's main station and the hub where the Western Line meets the Gulf Express. Travellers may say Abu Dhabi city or the capital.

## Dubai (DXB)
Dubai's main station. Note that DXB is the station code, not the airport.

## Sharjah (SHJ)
Serves Sharjah city. Also called Al Sharjah.

## Fujairah (FUJ)
The eastern terminus on the Gulf of Oman coast. Also written Al Fujairah.
