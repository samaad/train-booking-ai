# Lines and services

## Gulf Express (ER101, ER102)
The Gulf Express runs daily between Abu Dhabi and Fujairah, calling at Dubai and Sharjah. ER101 runs eastbound in the morning. ER102 runs westbound in the evening. It has Business and Economy classes.

## Western Line (ER201, ER202)
The Western Line runs daily between Al Ruwais and Abu Dhabi, calling at Al Mirfa. ER201 runs towards Abu Dhabi early in the morning and ER202 returns in the evening. It has Business and Economy classes.

## Night Coastal (ER301, ER302)
The Night Coastal is an overnight Economy-only service at weekends. ER301 leaves Al Ruwais late on Friday and Saturday evenings and reaches Abu Dhabi and Dubai after midnight, so its later stops fall on the next calendar day. ER302 runs in the other direction on Thursday and Sunday nights.

## Connections
To travel from the Western Line to the Gulf Express, change trains at Abu Dhabi. Always check the actual timetable with a search, because connection times differ by day.
